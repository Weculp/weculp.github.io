04 — Games · Infinite Tic-Tac-Toe

Tic-tac-toe, but it never ends. Each player keeps three pieces on the board — no more. Place a fourth and your oldest one vanishes. Adapt or lose.

Real-time two-player game over the web. Pick a mode, create a room, share the code, and play.

Game modes
Recommended · 3-3-3 rule

Classic Infinite

  1. Max 3 pieces per player on the board.
  2. Placing your 4th piece removes your oldest one.
  3. 3-in-a-row wins. No draws are possible.

Tight, fast, every move is a sacrifice · ~3–5 min per game

Variant · Overflow

Overflow

  1. Standard tic-tac-toe rules until the board fills up.
  2. When the board is full, the globally oldest piece is removed automatically.
  3. 3-in-a-row still wins. Longer, more chaotic games.

Closer to classic tic-tac-toe + tiebreaker · ~5–10 min per game
